# Band

The band runs across the whole width of a page, above the masthead, and says one thing of the whole
site that a reader must see before anything else, such as that it is still being built.

- Use `ss-band` as the first thing of the page, outside `ss-page`, holding one `p`: the plate.
- Its ground is stripes of `hazard` and `hazard-ink`, each `space-3` wide, slanting at 45 degrees, as on
  the tape round a building site. They are the one gradient of the style besides the ground's washes.
- The plate sets the words in the `type` face, bold, `hazard-ink` on `hazard` in a `border-line` of
  `hazard-ink`, so that they read the same in both themes. They keep to one line where the page is wide
  enough and wrap, centred, where it is not.
- Its words are not the component's: the place that uses it gives them, the site's brief on the site.
- A page has one band at most.
