# T25, stage 2, group 1: the final assessment

You review drafts of nodes of the SpecSheriff guidelines before the owner sees them. The owner will see each node once, in its final wording, on one page for the whole group. So a node you find ready must need no further change. Do not edit any file: your final message is your report.

The setting is the same as in `review2-brief-group1.md` in this folder. Read its sections "What the drafts are" and "How to review" first.

## What this review is for

Earlier reviews found these drafts ready, or found them ready once fixes that the author has since applied word for word were made. Since then, round e (`fix-group1-e.mjs`) corrected their English after the reports of EngStickler's readers. The round-e section of `group1-notes.md` lists each change. The owner's page shows each node with a block headed «Оценка», and no review has yet written that block for these drafts in their final wording. Your input file holds the drafts in their final wording.

For each draft:

1. Check that round e kept the meaning of the node's Russian wording. Where a proposal changes an agreed node, check that it kept the meaning of the agreed Russian as well.
2. Review the draft in full. Use the checklist that `node .claude/vt/writing-rules.mjs` prints, and search `specs/`, `moved/` and `tools-drafts.json` for each term the draft uses.
3. Write its «Оценка» block in Russian, with one line per criterion, named in the owner's words: согласованность, целостность, целесообразность, избыточность, недостаточность. Each line gives ✓ with the nodes the wording was compared with, or the problem found. For избыточность and недостаточность, ✓ means that none was found.
4. Where a sentence still needs a change, give the exact words to apply, in English and in Russian.

## Your report

In English, except for the «Оценка» blocks, which are in Russian. Keep it short. For each draft, give:

- its key;
- the verdict, **ready** or **not ready**;
- each finding, with the words concerned and the fix in the exact words to apply, marked either as one the author can fix or as a question only the owner can answer;
- every verdict line of the checklist;
- the «Оценка» block.

End with the list of findings that only the owner can answer, if there are any.
