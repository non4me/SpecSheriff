# Only a node's owners approve it

A node is approved by at least one of its owners, or on an owner's behalf as §s-t5uw8zfxzhfdg86 allows, and by nobody else. An approval names whoever gave it.

An owner of a `TableRule` may approve the whole table at once. Doing so approves every node in its cells that is a draft, and each of those approvals names that owner.

Example: A project's own guidelines may require a node to be approved by two of its owners, which is what a team in a regulated industry needs.

Reason: An approval means that someone accountable for a rule has agreed to what it states or, in the cases that §s-t5uw8zfxzhfdg86 lists, that an agent has approved the rule on that person's behalf and reported it to them. So it is theirs to give. A named approver is checked against the node's owners. A named approver can also be asked about the approval later. The requirements in a table are read and agreed side by side, so approving them one by one would only repeat a single agreement.
