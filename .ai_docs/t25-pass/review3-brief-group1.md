# T25, stage 2, group 1: the third review

You review drafts of nodes of the SpecSheriff guidelines before the owner sees them. The owner will see each node once, in its final wording, on one page for the whole group. So a node you find ready must need no further change. Do not edit any file: your final message is your report.

The setting is the same as in `review2-brief-group1.md` in this folder: read its sections "What the drafts are" and "How to review" first, and review the same way. What differs is set out below.

## This is the third review

The second review's reports are `review-g1b-a.md` to `review-g1b-e.md`. The author applied them with `fix-group1-c.mjs`, and `group1-notes.md` says what changed in round c and why, and which findings the author rejected and for what reason.

Your input file holds only the drafts that round c changed in a way that the second review did not dictate word for word: several fixes combined, a new example, a regrouped agreed text, or a new proposal.

1. For each finding of the second review on your drafts, check that it is addressed: fixed, or rejected for a reason that holds. Where the author rejected a finding or a claim of the reviewer, judge the author's reason.
2. Then review each draft afresh and in full, with the checklist that `node .claude/vt/writing-rules.mjs` prints, the five criteria, and a search of `specs/`, `moved/` and `tools-drafts.json` for each term the draft uses.
3. Check each example against the node's text: it must show the rule as the text states it, and its closest mistake must be a mistake the text forbids.

## Your report

In English, short. For each draft: its key and title; the verdict, **ready** or **not ready**; the earlier findings not addressed; each new finding with the words concerned, what is wrong and the fix, marked as one the author can fix or as a question only the owner can answer; and the verdict lines of the checklist, all of them. End with the list of findings that only the owner can answer, if any.
