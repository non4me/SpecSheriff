# T25, stage 2, group 2: the confirming review

You review drafts of nodes of the SpecSheriff guidelines before the owner sees them. The owner will see each node once, in its final wording, on one page for the whole group. So a node you find ready must need no further change. Do not edit any file: your final message is your report.

The setting is the same as in `review-brief-group2.md` in this folder. Read its sections "What the drafts are" and "How to review" first, and review the same way.

## What this review checks

The first review's reports are `review-g2-a.md` to `review-g2-e.md`. The author applied them, together with the reports of EngStickler's readers, with `fix-group2-b.mjs`. The round-b section of `group2-notes.md` says what changed, which findings the author rejected and why, and which sentences the author wrote in their own words. `english-group2-changed.md` lists those sentences. Your input file holds the drafts in their current wording.

For each draft:

1. Check that each fix that the first review gave for it is in place, word for word, or rejected for a reason that holds.
2. Check each sentence that round b wrote in the author's own words. It must keep the meaning of the node's Russian wording.
3. Review the draft afresh and in full, with the checklist that `node .claude/vt/writing-rules.mjs` prints and the five criteria.
4. Write its «Оценка» block in Russian, with one line per criterion, named in the owner's words: согласованность, целостность, целесообразность, избыточность, недостаточность. Each line gives ✓ with the nodes the wording was compared with, or the problem found.
5. Where a sentence still needs a change, give the exact words to apply, in English and in Russian.

For a key that begins with `proposal:`, check also its question in `group2-notes.md`. The question must be one only the owner can answer, and no earlier ruling may settle it. It must be put in terms of what a person sees, and each option must state what it costs. One case needs particular care: an option that would change what `docs/principles.md` says must say so in its cost, because the owner changes a principle first.

## Your report

In English, except for the «Оценка» blocks, which are in Russian. Keep it short. For each draft, give:
- its key, and the verdict: **ready** or **not ready**;
- each finding, with the words concerned and the fix in the exact words to apply, marked either as one the author can fix or as a question only the owner can answer;
- every verdict line of the checklist;
- the «Оценка» block.

End with the list of findings that only the owner can answer, if there are any.
